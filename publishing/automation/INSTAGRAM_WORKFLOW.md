## 3. Step-by-Step Instagram Collab Reel Upload Protocol

### Step 3.1: Asset Selection
1. Open the Instagram mobile app or Meta Business Suite logged into `@rtechlabofficial`.
2. Tap the **+** (Create) icon $\to$ select **Reel**.
3. Select the 9:16 short clip (Concept 1: *The 13,000 km Underwater Wire*, or the 60-second teaser).
4. Tap **Next** to reach the edit/preview screen.

### Step 3.2: Cover Photo Selection & Profile Grid Crop (CRITICAL)
1. Tap **Edit cover** on the video preview.
2. Select an explicit high-contrast still frame from the video OR upload a custom 1080×1920 cover image.
3. Tap **Profile grid** tab at the top.
4. Drag and adjust the 1:1 square crop box to ensure the headline text (e.g., *"13,000 KM UNDERWATER"* or *"HOW THE INTERNET WORKS"*) is perfectly centered and completely visible.
5. Tap **Done**.

### Step 3.3: Invite IANT Computer Education Palanpur as Collaborator
1. On the final share screen, tap **Tag people**.
2. Tap **Invite collaborator** (do NOT just tag as a photo tag).
3. In the search box, search for IANT Palanpur's official handle (e.g., `@iant_palanpur` or `@iantcomputereducation`).
4. Select the verified IANT Palanpur profile.
5. Tap **Done** (checkmark).
   > **Note:** Once IANT accepts the invitation, the Reel will show **"rtechlabofficial and iant_palanpur"** as joint authors, and the video will be shared directly to both audiences, pooling likes, comments, and views.
