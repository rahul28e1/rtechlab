## 3. Technical Verification & Fact Check Citations

1. **Subsea Cable Traffic Share:**  
   According to the TeleGeography Subsea Cable Map and ITU global telecom reports, over **99% of international intercontinental data traffic** travels via subsea fiber-optic cables, not satellites (satellites carry < 1%, primarily remote aviation and maritime links).
2. **Total Cable Length:**  
   Over **1.4 million kilometers** of commercial submarine telecommunications cables are deployed across global oceans.
3. **Speed of Light in Optical Fiber:**  
   Speed in vacuum $c \approx 300,000\text{ km/s}$. Refractive index of standard silica glass ($n \approx 1.4682$) causes speed $v = c / n \approx 204,000\text{ km/s}$ ($\approx 2/3\text{ c}$).
4. **Ashburn, Virginia Data Center Dominance:**  
   Loudoun County / Ashburn, VA ("Data Center Alley") routes an estimated **70% of global daily internet traffic** across dense hyperscale and colocation facilities.
