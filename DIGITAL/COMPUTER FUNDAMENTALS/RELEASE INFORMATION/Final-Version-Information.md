## 3. Targeted Technical Corrections Incorporated in V5.1.3

1. **Version & Imprint Synchronization:**
   - Standardized all covers, running footers, blueprint curriculums, and imprint metadata blocks strictly to `V5.1.3` and subject code `RTL-2027-FUND-V5.1.3` across all English and Hindi source manuscripts.
2. **Swollen Lithium Battery Life Safety Guidance:**
   - Removed unsafe instructions directing untrained users to handle, unseat, puncture, package in sand, or transport damaged batteries.
   - Enforced immediate stop-use protocol, AC power disconnection, non-combustible isolation in a cool and well-ventilated space, and mandatory contact with institutional Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) or certified hazardous waste disposal specialists for on-site management.
3. **NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 (Sept 2025) Storage Sanitization Standards:**
   - Updated citations from Rev. 1 to Rev. 2 across text, tables, MCQs, and bibliographic entries.
   - Correctly classified sanitization tiers:
     - **Clear:** Single-pass logical overwrite is sufficient for legacy magnetic HDDs, but not universally complete for flash SSDs due to Flash Translation Layer (FTL) wear leveling and over-provisioning.
     - **Purge:** Controller-level Sanitize block-erase and Cryptographic Erase (CE). Degaussing is strictly a Purge technique applicable solely to suitable magnetic media (HDDs, tapes) and is completely ineffective on solid-state flash memory.
     - **Destroy:** Physical substrate destruction rendering data recovery impossible (industrial cross-cut shredding to sub-2 mm particles, incineration, disintegration, or melting).
4. **Cooling Fan Cleaning Protocols:**
   - Removed unverified universal back-EMF damage claims.
   - Prescribed manufacturer-compliant maintenance: gently stabilize fan impellers using a clean non-conductive plastic probe during compressed air cleaning to prevent mechanical bearing over-rotation and blade fracture caused by high-velocity air streams exceeding designed RPM limits; avoid high-pressure direct blasts at the bearing hub.
5. **Component Cleaning & 99% IPA Qualification:**
   - Qualified that 99% anhydrous Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) is standard for gold edge connector fingers (RAM, PCIe), but cleaning must strictly follow component manufacturer guidance to avoid damaging delicate plastics, optical sensors, or thermal pads.
6. **Hindi Address Bus & DMA Parity:**
   - Updated Hindi Chapter 1 table, short QA, and MCQ explanations to include Direct Memory Access (DMA) bus-master exceptions where DMA controllers or peripheral devices take control of the Address Bus, matching English technical accuracy.
7. **Hindi Computing Generations Processing Speed Metrics:**
   - Replaced misleading picosecond and femtosecond instruction processing claims with physical clock cycle and operational throughput realities: sub-nanosecond clock cycles ($< 1$ ns / GHz frequencies) for 4th generation microprocessors, and multi-GHz / TOPS throughput for 5th generation AI NPUs.
8. **Devanagari Typography & Linguistic Verification:**
   - Rigorously audited all Hindi chapters for typography, conjuncts, and formatting parity, ensuring zero layout overflow and maintaining an exact 45-page compilation.
