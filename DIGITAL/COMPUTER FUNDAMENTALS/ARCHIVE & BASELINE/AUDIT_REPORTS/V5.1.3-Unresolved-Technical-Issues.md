## 3. Editorial Conventions Locked for Phase 4

To ensure total clarity during Phase 4 source manuscript modification, the following editorial conventions have been locked:
1. **DDR5 vs DDR4 Presentation:** DDR5 is presented as the primary architectural standard for the 2027 curriculum ($t_\text{REFW} = 32\text{ ms}, t_\text{REFI} = 3.9\text{ }\mu\text{s}$), with DDR4 ($64\text{ ms} / 7.8\text{ }\mu\text{s}$) explicitly identified as the historical predecessor. Density-specific $t_\text{RFC}$ values are presented per JEDEC Table 170.
2. **DNS Buffer Sizing:** The textbook presents 1232 bytes as a conservative fragmentation-avoidance recommendation (DNS Flag Day 2020) and 1400 bytes as a contextual resolver ceiling (RFC 9715), explaining the network engineering trade-offs without presenting either as a fixed protocol standard limit or zero-fragmentation guarantee.
3. **Storage Diagnostics:** The textbook presents `Win32_DiskDrive.Status` as the preliminary built-in command, explains its limitations, and introduces `Get-StorageReliabilityCounter` and `smartctl` for full attribute inspection.
4. **CMOS Dynamic Power:** Standardized on $P_\text{dynamic} = \alpha \cdot C_L \cdot V_{DD}^2 \cdot f$ with $\alpha$ explicitly defined as the $0 \to 1$ switching probability per cycle.
5. **PCIe 6.0 Bandwidth:** Standardized on 8 GB/s/lane simplex, 128 GB/s simplex, and 256 GB/s bidirectional raw maximum across x16, with qualified FLIT mode overhead.
