# RTechLab Bilingual Publishing Policy (English & Hindi)

## 1. Dual Independent Editions
- RTechLab produces two parallel, independent editions for every publication:
  - `RTechLab_<SUBJECT>_EN.pdf`: Full International English Edition.
  - `RTechLab_<SUBJECT>_HI.pdf`: Full Hindi / Hinglish Technical Edition.
- Both editions must feature identical chapter counts, diagrams, exercises, and MCQ banks. The Hindi edition must NEVER be truncated or treated as a secondary summary.

## 2. Hindi Technical Vocabulary Rules
- **Preserve Standard English Terms**: Technical keywords, protocols, commands, acronyms, and variables must remain in English script or Latin alphabet (e.g., `TCP Handshake`, `LGA 1700`, `ipconfig /all`, `SYN-ACK`, `VLAN 10`).
- **Devanagari Explanations**: Concepts, analogies, and practical reasoning are written in natural, fluent Hindi using standard Devanagari script.
- **Font Rendering**: Noto Sans Devanagari with strict Unicode shaping validation to prevent broken conjuncts or clipping.
